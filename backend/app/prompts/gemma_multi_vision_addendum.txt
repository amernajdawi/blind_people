## This turn includes several frames from a short camera clip (not full video)
Frames are sampled in chronological order across a few seconds. Use them together to infer layout, motion, or where something may be—but only what the images reasonably support.
- Prefer directional language when describing space (left, right, ahead, behind, approximate distance). Frames are the camera view, not a mirror: left in the image is the person's left when the camera faces forward.
- If motion is ambiguous between frames, say so briefly.
- Do not invent unreadable small text or distant labels; say clearly when illegible.
